01 / THE PLATFORM

More clarity at
every stage.

Tyre information is often scattered across inspections, workshops and recovery points. TYRELOOP brings those touchpoints into one traceable system.

By combining image-based inspection, experimental tread-depth measurement and digital lifecycle records, the platform helps users understand a tyre's condition and what should happen next.

04 / WHY IT MATTERS

Better decisions.

Tyres move through many hands during their useful life. When inspection and recovery information is disconnected, important details can be missed.

TYRELOOP aims to improve visibility across that journey, supporting more informed inspection practices and better documented recovery activities.
